About

I'm a specialist in programme delivery and team leadership, with a track record spanning Financial Services, Healthcare, Sport, Energy, Publishing and AI. I've worked in consultancies, agencies and product houses. Always in the most complex, highest-stakes corners of the business.

I founded and ran a digital consultancy for five years before moving into senior delivery roles, which means I understand commercial reality as well as craft. I know what it takes to build a business, not just manage a project.

I have also built and shipped my own SaaS products and AI agents, so I understand delivery from the inside out. I bring that into client work, designing agents and automated workflows that take the manual effort out of programme operations and give teams and leadership faster, clearer information.

What sets me apart is my ability to restore trust in struggling programmes, bring structure to chaos and grow the people around me. I've been promoted from every permanent role I've held. I've also hired and developed Project Managers who've gone on to significant careers.

I'm a certified Scrum Master and a certified Leader Who Coaches, mentored by a highly regarded PMI consultant. I bring methodology without rigidity and governance without bureaucracy.

How I Work

01

Clarity before velocity

I establish shared understanding before anything moves. That means proper discovery, stakeholder alignment and a prioritisation framework everyone trusts. Speed without clarity just creates expensive rework.

02

Governance that enables

Structure should accelerate delivery, not slow it down. I design governance that gives leadership visibility and teams autonomy, cutting through noise to focus on what genuinely matters for delivery.

03

People at the centre

The best programmes are built on trusted relationships. I invest heavily in team culture, coaching and communication. A motivated, aligned team consistently outperforms any process.

04

Build the tools, not just the plan

I bring AI into delivery directly, designing agents and automated workflows that remove repetitive work. I would rather build something that fixes a problem permanently than manage around it.
